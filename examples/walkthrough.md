# Example from first chat to application review

This is a fictional exercise. The listings and work history below are made up. Follow along in chat without opening an employer portal.

1. **Bring in useful context.** In a chat where you've discussed your work, paste the [career handoff prompt](../CHATGPT-HANDOFF-PROMPT.txt). Review the summary, correct it, and bring the work-related parts into your new job-finder chat. You can also start with a résumé and a conversation.
2. **Confirm an example.** The summary says you built a test-bench checklist. You confirm you wrote the checklist and introduced it to your shift. Your shift logs show nine test records with missing fields across 30 shifts before rollout and two across 30 shifts afterward. Keep the source and what you personally did in your experience log.
3. **Decide what to look for.** You want full-time test technician or equipment reliability work. Onsite or hybrid is fine within a 30-minute commute. Fully remote roles must support home-based work from your location without required travel. You set an $85,000 target and leave the minimum open. One coordinator can search both lanes. If you have subagents and want to broaden the search, one worker can cover local roles and another can cover eligible remote roles.
4. **Review the shortlist.** Two boards show the same tester role from Example Employer A, requisition DEMO-17. Keep one tracker entry and verify it on the employer's site in a real search. A second role requires weekly site visits beyond your travel and commute limits, so it stays off the shortlist.
5. **Review a tailored draft.** The tester posting emphasizes documentation, so the draft adds this bullet from your log:

   > Wrote and introduced a test-bench checklist. Test records with missing fields fell from nine to two across equal 30-shift periods.

   In a real application, you would review the full résumé, actual screening answers, destination and any terms before authorizing the assistant. An example approval would identify Example Employer A, DEMO-17, the reviewed résumé version and the specific submit action. This exercise ends at the preview.
