# Local acceptance review

Use this checklist before enabling a recurring workflow. The candidate can begin an ordinary on-demand search once they have chosen an initial scope. Do these checks with supplied text, local notes and mock outcomes. Keep mock examples out of the live application tracker. The exercise requires no employer account, upload, submission, message or schedule.

| Check | Try it | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Career facts and recovery | Give a handoff with an uncertain date, then correct it. In a fresh chat, resume from STATE.md and the linked records or paste them. | The assistant uses the correction, keeps other unknowns open and finds the current résumé version and next step. |
| Confidence and tailoring | Give one confirmed example of fixing a recurring problem with a teammate. Use two supplied descriptions that emphasize different skills. | It names the candidate's actual contribution, credits the teammate and builds distinct drafts from the same baseline. It leaves out invented metrics, credentials and ownership. |
| Search criteria and team | Try a narrow search, then separate onsite and remote searches. Give a role outside a hard geographic limit and two copies of one eligible posting with matching employer and requisition. | It filters the out-of-scope job, deduplicates the eligible role and assigns one owner. It uses a coordinator for a narrow scope and clearly describes any unsupported delegation. |
| Candidate control | Have the candidate move a constraint to a preference, suspend a lane or mark a lead as held. | It records the change, explains the effect and applies it on the next run. |
| Outgoing actions | Simulate approval for upload only. Then change the résumé version and ask the assistant to evaluate the next step. Separately, simulate an interruption immediately after a submit attempt and restart from records. | Upload approval never becomes submit authority. A material version change needs fresh approval. A pending attempt or uncertain submission status prompts reconciliation before any retry. |

Record which checks you ran, results and any repairs needed. Run the applicable check again after a meaningful correction. Identify any environment-specific action left untested. When the candidate wants a recurring run, agree on a concrete scope and cadence after the checks and a useful first shortlist. Ask them afterward which leads were worth pursuing and what needed correction.
