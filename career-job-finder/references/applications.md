# Tailor, review and apply

**Applications and messages go out in your name. You are responsible for what you authorize the assistant to send, the accuracy of your answers and any terms you agree to. Review them as carefully as if you were sending them yourself.**

## Prepare a role packet

1. Verify the exact role or mark its availability unresolved. Check the tracker and known employer application history for duplicates. Use the candidate's approved baseline résumé and relevant confirmed examples from the experience log. If no baseline exists, draft one with them and preserve the approved version.
2. Tailor emphasis, ordering and language to the actual role. Keep dates, qualifications, skill scope, results and team attribution accurate. Use the employer's words when they accurately describe their work. Do not guess screening answers, fill skill gaps with keywords, invent metrics or imply a credential. Support a stretch recommendation privately with a specific explanation.
3. Keep a role folder under applications/ with source and versioned résumé, supporting text and review notes. Name the baseline and briefly explain meaningful changes. Inspect the final output the employer will see. Read final text for accuracy and consistency. When producing a formatted document, inspect every rendered page and verify important extracted text where supported. If a check or format is unavailable, state the limit and offer a usable alternative.
4. Present the meaningful fit and gaps, exact role and route, reviewed files and answers, and any unanswered questions or terms. Distinguish a local packet, an entered form, a verified saved draft and a confirmed submission. A local packet can be useful before the form is fully accessible.

## Authorize only the action on the table

- **Prepare.** “This looks good” may be feedback on a draft. Local drafting does not authorize account creation, disclosure, contact, upload or submission.
- **Set up or enter.** When tools and employer rules permit, the coordinator can create an employer account with specific approval for that site and action, or reuse an existing account where authorized. Obtain specific permission before entering or uploading candidate information, which may save or transmit it before submission. Show what will go where and whether any visible terms are involved. Get missing answers directly. Have the candidate handle passwords, verification codes, CAPTCHAs and steps reserved to them. Use a manual route where tooling or portal design requires it.
- **Submit or send.** Show the final employer, exact role or recipient, attachments and version, answers or message text, and actual terms or certification language if present. Request one clear instruction for the exact action or identified batch. Approval for entry does not transfer to submission. A material change to files, facts, recipients or terms invalidates the approval for the changed action. Show the change and get fresh approval. If a form requires the candidate’s own signature, certification or other personal step, hand it to them. When delegated acceptance is allowed, obtain their specific authorization after they can review the terms. Treat email as draft-and-review by default. Use an approved sending tool only after the candidate explicitly authorizes that message or batch and the available permissions allow it. If they send it themselves, record a reported send when they tell you.
- **Commitments.** The candidate decides about interviews, offers or other commitments. An application approval gives no permission to accept an offer, spend money or make a separate promise.

Example approval language after a complete review:

> I authorize you to submit the reviewed application for [employer and requisition] using [exact file/version] and the answers we just reviewed.

If terms or another distinct action are bundled into the final control, specify that separately in the review and approval. The assistant may help explain what is on screen and give the candidate a chance to read the original text. It does not decide consent for them.

## Execute and reconcile

Before any approved action, check the route, role and reviewed version again. Save the scope and time of the candidate’s approval and the exact payload or version references under the role record. Immediately before an external action, record **Attempt pending confirmation**, the action, destination, version and time. For a submission, also mark the tracker **Submission attempt pending confirmation**. Then perform the approved action when supported. If the session ends or restarts while that marker is present, reconcile the external status first. The marker means an attempt may have occurred. Check the actual status or have the candidate verify it before retrying. If you cannot retain this checkpoint, hand the final action to the candidate. Pause for required candidate participation, missing information or any meaningful change. If a portal disallows automation or has no supported route, provide the exact files, answers and next click so the candidate can complete it themselves.

Verify each submission through the actual employer confirmation, receipt or application history, or record what the candidate reports and label that source. Save the time, confirmation ID if available, exact material versions and any limits. If the outcome is uncertain, write **Submission outcome unknown** and check the existing record or receipt before considering a retry. Never count a Submit click alone as confirmation. Keep drafts, rejected applications and prior submissions in their original role folders. Update the tracker with what changed and the next action.
