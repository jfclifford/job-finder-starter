Job Finder Starter

1. Download the ZIP and unzip it somewhere you control.
2. If you already have Codex, open that folder in Codex and paste
   START-ONBOARDING.txt into a new chat. Codex reads the project guides.
3. If you're new to Codex, paste CHATGPT-HANDOFF-PROMPT.txt into a
   ChatGPT conversation where you've talked about your work. It can
   guide you through setup and ask which career details to bring over.
   If it asks for a guide, give it career-job-finder/references/setup-codex.md.
   You can also start with a resume or answer questions directly.

You choose the kinds of work, places and constraints. The assistant helps
you turn real work examples into a resume, checks job postings and
adapts drafts for roles you choose. You review and approve any outgoing
application or message in your name.

Keep your private materials in these folders in your local copy:
  records/       Career profile, experience log and tracker
  resumes/       Resume files you bring to the workspace
  applications/ Per-role evidence, drafts, approvals and receipts
  research/      Working search evidence

Git ignores these folders by default. Before sharing or committing
changes, check that your personal information is only in your private
copy. For a ChatGPT-only option and a fictional example, see README.md.
